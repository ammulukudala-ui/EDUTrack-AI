About EDUTrack AI

Students often don’t know which subjects need attention, and faculty find it hard to spot who may need support when information is scattered. EDUTrack AI brings records together and offers transparent, rule-based analysis with clear explanations. Scores are advisory and designed to support students — never to predict failure or judge anyone.
